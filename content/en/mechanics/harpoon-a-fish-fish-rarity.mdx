## Depth and Zones: The Biggest Rarity Lever

If you only optimize one thing for **Harpoon a Fish fish rarity**, make it depth. Deeper zones pull from rarer fish pools, and that single variable outweighs almost everything else you can control.

| Depth Band | Fish Pool Feel | Rarity Expectation | Main Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shallows | Starter creatures, high volume | Common to Uncommon | Almost none |
| Mid water | Mixed pool, first real variety | Uncommon to Rare | Occasional tough catches |
| Deep water | Heavier creatures, slower bites | Rare to Epic | Gear and level checks |
| Abyss / endgame | Monster-class pool | Epic to Legendary+ | Failed attempts, time sink |

Community reports consistently point to the same pattern: players who linger in the shallows for comfort end up with a full but *boring* aquarium. The moment they push one zone deeper than feels safe, their rarity spread changes dramatically.

A few rules of thumb worth internalizing:

- **Descend one zone at a time.** Jumping straight to the abyss with mid-tier gear wastes attempts.
- **Bank EXP before you dive.** Extra levels mean stronger harpoons, which means tougher catches become possible.
- **Fish the deepest zone you can clear reliably**, not the deepest one you can technically reach.
- **Track your last ten catches.** If nothing above Uncommon appeared, you're too shallow.

The relationship between depth and rarity is the closest thing this game has to a guarantee — but it only pays off if your gear can actually land what bites.

Practically, no. Even if a rare creature bites, you need the gear and level to land it. Treat your harpoon tier as the hard ceiling on your **Harpoon a Fish fish rarity** results.

**Why do I keep catching the same fish over and over?**
Usually it's a sign you're fishing too shallow for your level, or you've stopped rotating zones. Change depth, log your next fifteen catches, and compare the tier spread.

**Do promo codes help with rarity?**
Indirectly, yes. Codes grant EXP and Ruby Keys, EXP unlocks stronger harpoons faster, and stronger harpoons raise the rarity ceiling you can actually collect.

**Is there a guaranteed way to force a rare catch?**
No — the system is a roll, not a recipe. The best you can do is stack the odds: deepest reliable zone, best harpoon you own, full level, and consistent rotation.
