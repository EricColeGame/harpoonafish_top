## Where Ruby Keys Fit in Your Upgrade Plan

Ruby Keys are the most misunderstood resource in the game. They don't buy upgrades directly — they open bonus rewards that stock your aquarium and push your collection closer to completion. That makes them a collection accelerator, not a power shortcut.

The practical implication: don't hoard them waiting for a perfect moment that never arrives, but don't dump them all in one sitting either. Spend in batches, right after you unlock a new depth zone, so the rewards land on top of your newest creature pool. Player experience suggests this feels noticeably better than spending them in the starting area.
