## How Harpoon a Fish Exotic Fish Spawn: Depth, Luck, and Harpoon Power

Catching a Harpoon a Fish exotic fish comes down to stacking odds in your favor. No single trick guarantees one, but the factors below compound quickly when you handle all of them at once.

| Factor | What it changes | How to improve it |
|---|---|---|
| Depth | Which creatures are even in the pool | Push one zone deeper every session |
| Harpoon tier | Whether a heavy catch can be reeled in at all | Spend EXP on upgrades before spending on looks |
| Boost items | How often rare rolls come up | Save boosts for deep runs, not shallow warm-ups |
| Session length | Total number of catch attempts per hour | Fewer, longer, focused dives beat scattered ones |
| Server choice | Competition for the same spawns | Player experience suggests quieter servers feel more productive |

Depth is the single biggest lever. The game is built around sinking further for better finds, so a player with a mid-tier harpoon at max depth will usually outperform a player with a great harpoon still fishing the shallows. Gear raises your ceiling; depth decides what is available to you in the first place.

The second lever is patience with the reel. Mashing the input to speed things up is the most common reason a promising catch slips away — community reports consistently describe losing big creatures to rushed reels rather than to bad luck.

## Step-by-Step: Catching a Harpoon a Fish Exotic Fish

If you want a repeatable routine instead of random luck, follow this order. It mirrors how experienced players structure a session.

1. **Bank your EXP first.** Redeem any live codes and finish easy catches until you level. Higher levels unlock stronger harpoons, and a stronger harpoon is what lets you handle tougher monsters sooner.
2. **Upgrade the harpoon before anything cosmetic.** Every level you spend on pulling power pays you back in catches per hour.
3. **Dive deeper than last time.** Even one zone deeper changes the pool of creatures available to you.
4. **Watch the approach, not the button.** Learn the visual tell for a heavy creature so you are not surprised mid-reel.
5. **Reel steadily and let the line hold.** If the catch fights, ease off instead of forcing it.
6. **Get it into the aquarium immediately.** A banked fish is a permanent collection entry; an unbanked one is still a risk.
7. **Evolve what you keep.** Evolution is where a decent catch turns into a genuine monster.

| Step | Time cost | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Redeeming codes and leveling | A few minutes | Faster access to better harpoons |
| Harpoon upgrades | Ongoing | More successful reels per run |
| Deeper dives | Per session | Access to rarer creature pools |
| Aquarium banking and evolution | Short | Permanent collection progress |
