## Etiquette and Pro Tips for the Harpoon a Fish Discord Community

New members who lurk quietly for a week learn more than members who post ten questions on day one. A few habits that make the server work for you:

- **Search first, ask second.** Nearly every "which harpoon next" question has been answered a dozen times.
- **Screenshot your stats.** When asking for upgrade advice, post your level, current harpoon, and Ruby Key count — you will get a real answer instead of a guess.
- **Never share account details.** No legitimate moderator needs your Roblox password, and no giveaway requires a login.
- **Report codes that fail.** If a code is dead, say so politely; moderators update pinned lists based on those reports.
- **Give back after you progress.** The player who answers depth questions at level 40 was asking them at level 10.

Community reports also flag a recurring frustration: codes get posted in general chat, buried in thirty messages within a minute, and expire before anyone sees them. That is why announcement and codes channels exist — and why pinning them is the single highest-value thing you can do in your first five minutes on the server.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Harpoon a Fish Discord

**Is there an official Harpoon a Fish Discord server?**
Yes. The game's Roblox listing links directly to a Discord server and a Trello board, which makes those the two safest community resources to trust. Always join through the Roblox page rather than a link posted by a stranger.

**Do codes get posted in the Harpoon a Fish Discord before anywhere else?**
Often, yes — the codes guide explicitly recommends joining the server because developers message members directly when fresh promo codes or updates go live. Aggregator sites usually catch up shortly after.

**What should I do if a code from the server does not work?**
Double-check spelling and capitalization first, then wait a few minutes and retry. If it still fails, the code has likely expired or hit its redemption cap — report it in the codes channel so the pinned list gets updated.

**Can I get banned for asking too many questions?**
Not for asking, but for spamming. Read the rules channel, keep questions in the right channel, and avoid repeating the same post. Most servers only act on obvious spam, scams, or trading rule violations.

**Is the Harpoon a Fish Discord useful if I just started?**
It is most useful early, honestly. New players burn EXP on the wrong harpoons and miss limited codes entirely. An hour in the server can save you days of inefficient grinding — and once you are deep, you can pay it back by helping the next wave of anglers.
